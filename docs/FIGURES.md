All have PNG and vector PDF versions. Captions are not embedded.

Suggested paper captions:

**Figure 2.** Adjusted associations between self-reported SLD diagnoses and corresponding Vinegrad+ scales, in standard-deviation units. Bars indicate 95% confidence intervals.

**Figure 3.** Model-estimated changes in accuracy with TTS support across the eight exercises. Bars indicate 95% confidence intervals.

For optional probability/difference plots, “model-estimated” means conditional at random effects equal to zero. Profile and diagnosis plots must be read alongside the omnibus/interaction tests. Different significance of simple effects does not establish moderation. All plotted intervals are unadjusted 95% intervals.
